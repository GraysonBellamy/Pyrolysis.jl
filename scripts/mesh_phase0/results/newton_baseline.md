## (d) Findings

1. On the Eulerian decks the exact and reduced Jacobians are the same matrix, so their KenCarp4 rows are identical (4 of 12 exact/reduced pairs identical in Newton iterations and failures); on the ALE decks the exact Jacobian does not reduce the Newton iterations per accepted step, which stay within 27.3-32.1 for both strategies.
2. On the 8 ALE pairs the exact Jacobian has fewer nonlinear failures than the reduced one in 4, the same number in 3 and more in 1 (exact vs reduced: ale/25/sparspak 36 vs 62; ale/25/umfpack 36 vs 62; ale/100/sparspak 198 vs 239; ale/100/umfpack 198 vs 227; ale_darcy/25/sparspak 10 vs 10; ale_darcy/25/umfpack 12 vs 10; ale_darcy/100/sparspak 12 vs 12; ale_darcy/100/umfpack 12 vs 12), at 5.8-9.9x the reduced median time at 100 cells.
3. By median wall time on the six decks (reduced Jacobian, sparspak) the integrators rank FBDF (mean rank 1.17, first on 5/6), KenCarp4 (mean rank 2.50, first on 1/6), TRBDF2 (mean rank 2.50, first on 0/6), Rodas5P (mean rank 3.83, first on 0/6). By accepted steps the integrators rank KenCarp4 (mean rank 1.00, first on 6/6), TRBDF2 (mean rank 2.17, first on 0/6), FBDF (mean rank 3.33, first on 0/6), Rodas5P (mean rank 3.50, first on 0/6).
4. By maximum temperature error against the tight exact-Jacobian reference (abstol 1e-10, reltol 1e-8) the integrators rank FBDF (mean rank 2.17, first on 1/6), Rodas5P (mean rank 2.33, first on 3/6), TRBDF2 (mean rank 2.67, first on 1/6), KenCarp4 (mean rank 2.83, first on 1/6). Rodas5P takes one Jacobian per step (njacs = steps) and no Newton iterations; on the burnout deck TRBDF2 needs 1114 steps and 133 rejects against KenCarp4's 810 and 3, at 2.78 vs 3.65 s.
