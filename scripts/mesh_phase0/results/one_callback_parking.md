# One-callback parking

Generated by `scripts/mesh_phase0/baselines/one_callback_parking.jl` (shared helpers in `common.jl`); data in `one_callback_parking.csv`, `one_callback_parking_qois.csv` and `one_callback_parking_ledger.csv`; log alongside. Run as `julia --project=test --startup-file=no --threads=1 <script>` from the repository root.

Variants of the depletion callback (`create_depletion_callback`, replaced in-process): `current` = package method (at most one cell per invocation); `current_override` = the replacement with its loop disabled (must reproduce `current`); `one_callback` = the replacement looping `deplete!` while it returns `CELL_PARKED` **and** the callback's own event predicate `has_event(u)` (a depletion candidate exists, or on a fixed mesh an interior void) still holds, then one `derivative_discontinuity!(integrator, true)` and one `set_proposed_dt!(integrator, DT_AFTER_PARK)`; `STOP_SOLVE` still terminates. The predicate is re-tested before every further `deplete!` because `deplete!` returns `STOP_SOLVE` at `n_live <= min_cells` before it scans for a candidate: a loop that called it once more after a cascade leaving exactly `min_cells` live cells would terminate at the cascade instead of at the next threshold crossing. Each case at its CI cell count (V-BO-04: 40, the build default) through its own `solve_fn`/`solve_kwargs`; `V-BO-04/min20` is V-BO-04 with `min_cells = 20` (expected retcode `Terminated`: the first cascade leaves 20 live cells and the callback must stop the solve at the next crossing). `maxiters = 200000`; wall-clock guard 300.0 s. `t_last` = integrator time of the last accepted step (the termination time for `Terminated` runs); `last park` = time of the last park. Park invocations of `current` equal its park count by construction; the other two are counted in the override. Warm time = median of 3 solves after one warm-up; indicative (shared machine, another Julia process running concurrently). Mass ledger = |Δm − ∫ṁ″dt| / Δm with the integral a trapezoid of the sampled MLR on the output times (`_vbo_measures`), the same quantity as the "mass ledger closure" QoI of V-BO-01/02; it is a sampled diagnostic on the output grid, not a conservation statement (see the output-spacing check below).

## Termination with min_cells = 20 (V-BO-04/min20)

Every cell of V-BO-04 shrinks as exp(−r t/ρ_s) and the depletion reference is the initial cell thickness, so the k-th cascade crosses the 0.05 threshold at t_k = ln(2^k · 20) ρ_s/r = 299.573, 368.888, 438.203 s. The first cascade leaves exactly 20 live cells; at the second crossing `deplete!` must find `n_live <= min_cells` and stop the solve. A `DiscreteCallback` detects a crossing on the first accepted step after it, so the recorded termination time is the crossing plus up to one step.

| variant | retcode | t_last s | t_last − t₂ s | last step s | last park s | park invocations | cells parked | live cells end | steps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| current | Terminated | 370.330243 | 1.4423 | 3.61475 | 299.981487 | 20 | 20 | 20 | 236 |
| current_override | Terminated | 370.330243 | 1.4423 | 3.61475 | 299.981487 | 20 | 20 | 20 | 236 |
| one_callback | Terminated | 368.977246 | 0.0893 | 0.413451 | 299.981468 | 1 | 20 | 20 | 210 |

Both variants terminate at the second crossing (t₂ = 368.888 s) with 20 / 20 live cells after 20 / 20 parks: `current` on its first accepted step after t₂ at 370.330243 s (1.4423 s after the crossing, last step 3.61475 s), `one_callback` at 368.977246 s (0.0893 s after, last step 0.413451 s). The 1.353 s between the two recorded times is within one accepted step of each variant, i.e. the discrete callback's detection delay on different step sequences (the cascade takes 20 steps of DT_AFTER_PARK in `current` and one in `one_callback`), not a different termination event. A loop without the predicate re-test (the submitted version) terminated at the first cascade instead, at 299.981468 s (reviewer's probe); the corrected loop does not.
